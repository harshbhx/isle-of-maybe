Press kit

Isle of Maybe is a daily treasure-map game that runs inside Reddit posts. Every day there's a new 3D island, the same for everyone. Players follow a map another player drew from memory, find the chest before the tide comes in, then bury their own and chart it for the next sailor.

It's made by Harsh Bhanushali for Reddit's Game of the Day Hackathon (December 2026), on Reddit's Developer Platform. It's an independent game, not made or endorsed by Reddit.

Facts

  • Platform: Reddit posts (Devvit Web). It plays on phones and computers with no install.
  • Made with: React, three.js and a Redis-backed server. Fonts are Fraunces and Bricolage Grotesque.
  • First island: 5 December 2026, on r/IsleOfMaybe.
  • Contact: modmail to r/IsleOfMaybe, or an issue on GitHub.
