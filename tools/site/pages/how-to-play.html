How to play

Open today's island post on r/IsleOfMaybe and tap Set sail. Playing for the first time? Practice Cove walks you through it in five quick steps, and you can skip it.

A day on the island

  1. Pick a chart. Trusted is rated well by other sailors, Fresh is one nobody has tried yet, and the Old Captain's is always there, even before anyone else has played.
  2. Walk the island. You're the explorer, starting at the dock. The mist lifts where you walk. Open the chart whenever you like.
  3. Beat the tide. You have 150 seconds before the sea floods the low beach in three waves.
  4. Dig. You have three shovels. Dig where you think the X means.
  5. See the truth. Find the chest and it bursts open with its burier's name on the plaque. Miss, and you see where it really was against the map's X. Then rate the chart: True map or Cursed map.
  6. Bury your chest on a spot you've walked near.
  7. Chart it from memory. Up to six landmark stamps, a dotted path of up to eight steps, one X, and, if you like, one margin note from a fixed list ("Count your steps.").
  8. Check your ink. Your Ink check grades the chart against the real island right away. Tomorrow your Dispatch says how many sailors tried your chart and how many found your chest.

You can hunt up to three charts a day and bury one chest.

The ground

  • The tide covers the lowest sand first. You can't dig on flooded sand, so if the X is near the water, go there first.
  • Quicksand traps you for a couple of seconds.
  • Thicket, grass between jungle trees, slows you to half speed.
  • Cliffs, jungle and ponds block the way. Find a path around.

Gold and depth

A chest's depth is how far it is from the nearest landmark. The further out you bury, the harder it is to chart, and the more it pays.

DepthDistance from a landmarkPays
Shallow2 tiles or fewer×1
Buried3 to 4 tiles×2
Deep5 tiles or more×3
  • Finding a chest pays depth × (40 + 20 for each shovel you didn't use), plus 20 if you took a fairly direct route.
  • Your own chart earns 10 × depth in salvage gold every time someone finds your chest.
  • Ink check grades: Sharp, Steady, Wobbly or Cursed, from how close your stamps and X are to the real thing.
  • Ranks come from all-time finds: Deckhand, Mapmaker (5), Navigator (25), Cartographer (75) and Legend of the Isles (200). Your rank is the hat your explorer wears.

Every day is a little different

Each island is dressed as one of six biomes: Tropical, Volcanic, Autumn, Mangrove, Frost or Coral. The weekday changes the rules:

DayWeatherWhat changes
MondayClear skiesYou can see 4 tiles around you.
TuesdayTwinsSome landmarks have a double. Chart carefully.
WednesdayLow tideThe island is bigger.
ThursdayThick jungleMore paths are blocked.
FridaySea fogYou can see 2 tiles. Trust the map.
SaturdayLantern nightLandmarks near you glow through the dark.
SundayClear skiesA calm day.

When the community finds enough chests in a day (the crew goal), tomorrow becomes a Lantern night as a reward. The top reply under each day's sticky comment names tomorrow's island.

Together, on Reddit

  • Other redditors hunting the same island right now walk past as lantern-lit ghosts with their usernames. Tap Ghosts: on to turn them off. That works both ways: you see none, and nobody sees yours.
  • Today's footsteps wear paths into the grass once three or more hunts cross a spot. They're counts only, never one person's route.
  • The dock sign names whoever named the island.
  • Streaks, gold boards and the Hall of Charts reward coming back and drawing honest maps.

Fair play

One account each, no bots, and no chest spoilers in comments. Charts can be reported, and three reports hide a chart until a moderator looks. The full rules are in the Terms of Use.
