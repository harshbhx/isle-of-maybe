Trust a stranger's map.

A new 3D island every day, the same for everyone. Follow a map another player drew from memory, find their chest before the tide comes in, then bury yours and draw the way for the next sailor.

Play on r/IsleOfMaybe How to play

One island. One day. Every map drawn from memory.

A round takes about three minutes. Nobody sees the chest until they've found it, and nobody draws a map with the island in front of them.

  1. Pick a stranger's chart

    Trusted, Fresh, or the Old Captain's. Each is stamps, a dotted path and one X, drawn by another redditor after their own hunt.

  2. Beat the tide

    Walk and run the island with a thumb-stick or WASD. The sea floods the low beach in three waves. Quicksand waits. You have three shovels.

  3. Bury yours

    Pick a spot you've walked near. Far from landmarks is Deep, and pays three times as much to you and to whoever finds it.

  4. Chart it from memory

    Stamp what you remember, draw the path, mark the X. Tomorrow your Dispatch says how many sailors your map led to gold.

Dig where the X says.

Find it and the chest bursts open with its burier's name on the plaque. Miss, and you see exactly where it was against the map you trusted. Then you rate it: True map, or Cursed.

Six kinds of island.

Each day's island is cut from the same seed for everyone, and dressed as one of six biomes. On weekdays the weather changes the rules too: twin landmarks, sea fog, a bigger island at low tide.

Fair by design.

It's a game about trust, so it has to be honest underneath.

The server decides

Every step and every dig is checked on the server. The chest's position never reaches your phone until your hunt is over.

Maps, not messages

Charts are fixed stamps, a snapped path and one X. There's no free text or drawing anywhere, and every chart shows who made it.

Nothing follows you

No ads, analytics or trackers, in the game or on this site. Game data expires within 30 days, and you can delete your chart any time. Privacy policy.
